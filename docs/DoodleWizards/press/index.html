About the game

You're a wizard doodled on a notebook page, and the page is trying to kill you. Draw spells, stack silly combos, and survive waves of pencil-and-crayon enemies. It's a 3D game, played one stage at a time on a 10-minute clock.

  • Cast spells by drawing them. A spiral is a Dust Devil, a hook is a Rainbow Streak.
  • Eight wizards, each a different art tool, like Pencil, Chalk, Paint and Marker. Each has its own spells.
  • Level up and pile on spells until the page is covered.
  • Four stages, each with its own boss: the Eraser King, the Electric Sharpener, the Inkwell and the Crayon Box Dragon.
